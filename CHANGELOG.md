# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Plants know their size and where they fit: `PlantSpecies.matureHeight`, `spread` and `hang` (metres), `heightAt(growth)`, and `fit(surface, pot, growth)` returning a `PlantFit` (`Fits`, `TooTall`, `TooWide`, `TooDeep`, `HangsTooFar`) for a `Surface(width, depth, clearanceAbove, hangRoom)` and a `PotSize`. Checked at full size by default so a plant that fits now will not meet the board above as it grows.
- Sounds for liquids, and which sound goes with which action: `LiquidCue` / `LiquidCueKind` (`Stream`, `Splash`, `Gulp`, `Drip`, `SetDown`, `Knock`, `Boil`, `Soak`) and `LiquidEvents` in core (a vessel's `events` is told what it does; no listener, no cost); `LiquidSounds` in `boitata-audio-synth` (gulp, drip, splash, stream, soak, boil, set down, knock, made in code, seeded, the loops seamless); `LiquidSoundMap` (the default sound for each kind, or a `PcmBuffer` of your own with `set`, `setMuted`, `setGain`) and `LiquidAudioPlayer` / `LiquidAudio` in `boitata-audio` (one-off sounds scheduled on the sink's clock, stereo position from where the vessel is, continuous ones as loops that follow the level and fade). `Fluid.toxicity`, `LiquidVessel.materialTone` (glassy to dull). Playground: the Liquids tab plays them with Sound on, with a Sounds row to mute any kind.
- Watering plants: `Planter(pot, patch)` in `boitata-plants`: a pot is a `LiquidVessel` whose bottom is soil (`LiquidVessel.solid`), so what is poured in stands on the soil up to the rim (and runs over it) and soaks in at the soil's pace (sand fast, clay slow), raising `SoilMoisture`; soil wetter than field capacity drains out of the hole in the bottom (`takeDrained`). `PlantFluids` says what a liquid does to roots, from its numbers (`Fluid.toxicity`, new: water 0, coffee and tea a little, wine and oil a lot, kerosene kills; cloudy and thick liquids are less water), `SoilMoisture.contamination` and `saturatedHours` add poison and drowning to the plant's stress, and a thirsty plant perks up when watered. `LiquidVessel.solid` / `surfaceHeight`, `VesselShape.Pot` and `VesselStyle.soil` (`SoilLook`: the soil in the opening darkens when wet). Playground: a fern pot on the bench, watered by the jar, the jug, the tumbler, the wine and the kerosene ("Water the Fern pot" or hold to water), Wait an hour / Wait half a day chips; the bench's scale is a little smaller to fit it.
- Sipping from a point of view: `SipJob` brings the container in toward the camera (`Raise`: it grows and its top leans in to the eye, so the opening is seen from above), takes a gulp at once and one every half second while the sip is held (`Drink`; it leans in a little more as there is less in it), and puts it back on release, when it is empty, or when it is not safe to drink (`Lower`). `SipViewpoint` is how it is seen (`PovViewpoint`: big, low in the middle of the view, the scene dimmed and its edges darkened), `Drinker` says who is sipping (`Drinker.You` for now; another character in another camera is another viewpoint), `SipPose` is where the container is drawn, `drawVesselAt` and `drawSipOverlay` draw it. Playground: pressing Hold to sip brings the mug up to the camera; a Who sips row on the Liquids tab.
- Hold to sip, hold to pour, dribbles and overflow: `VesselAction.hold` and `VesselWheel(onHold = ...)` (press and release; the wheel stays up while held), `Sipper` (a gulp at once, then one every half second while held), `PourLevel.UntilStopped` poured for as long as the button is held, then the source goes home. A pour that goes on into a full container makes it run over, and it stops spilling once it is level with the brim (the last film runs off at once, not as a long weir tail). `LiquidVessel.dribble` / `takeDribbleDrop`: after a spill stops a little clings to the lip and lets go in beads (falling to the table or into another container, or running down the side to wet the foot); `drawVessel` draws the bead.
- Pouring that respects the container: `PourSide` (`Right`, `Left`, `Either`) on `LiquidVessel` says which way it can be tipped, and `TippingBody.allowedSide` enforces it (a knock toward the blocked side does nothing, a hand cannot turn it that way, it never rests past upright toward it). `PourJob` stands on the side the vessel pours from, hopping over the target if it has to. The tilt follows what is left (`Tilt.angleToReachLip`: a full vessel needs little, a nearly empty one a lot) with a flow control on top, and the job ends when the source is as drained as it gets. `PourLevel` (`Shot`, `Half`, `Amount(share)`, `TopUp`, `UntilStopped`) says how much to pour and `PourJob.stop()` ends it any time. `Fluid.potable` and `LiquidVessel.sippable`. Playground: the wheel offers Sip (potable liquids), Pour a shot, Top up, Pour until stopped and Stop pouring; the wine bottle tips only right, the milk jug only left.
- Sipping: `LiquidVessel.sip(share)` takes a mouthful (a twelfth of the container by default, or what is left), lowers the level, ripples the surface and returns what was taken. It only lowers the level for now (no tilt, no limit for hot drinks). Pouring into a full container already spilled the extra over the lip; now pinned by a test. Playground: a Sip chip on the Liquids tab.
- The moon follows the calendar and changes smoothly: `MoonPhase.of(clock: DayClock, ageAtStartDays)` gives the phase for a day (a 29.53-day month, so it changes every hour and repeats), `MoonPhase.age` and `MoonPhase.ofAge` put every phase on one circle, and `rememberSmoothMoon(target)` eases the shown moon into a newly picked phase the short way round the month instead of jumping (`MoonPhase.stepAge` is the pure step). Playground: a Calendar chip on the moon row (the default; picking a phase by hand still works), and the Garden's days drive it.
- Pouring from one container into another: `PourJob` (a hand slides the source beside the target, lifts it over the rim, tips it until the liquid runs over the lip at a steady rate, keeps the stream on the mouth, then sets it upright and carries it home; what misses is `missed`, for a puddle), `TippingBody.hold` / `release` (a held container turns smoothly and cannot fall over), `LiquidVessel.lift` / `landHeight` / `receive`, `Fluid.mix` (colour, thickness, cloudiness and weight averaged by volume: milk into coffee), `VesselAction`, `VesselShape.Jar`, and `VesselWheel`, a springy radial menu of choices over a container. `drawVessel`'s `pivotY` is now `floorY` (the vessel is drawn raised by its lift). Playground: tap a container for its wheel (Heat, Pour into ..., Drop in, Knock over); a coffee jar that pours into the mug, the milk jug into the mug, the wine and the kerosene into the tumbler.
- Plants that live with the weather: new module `boitata-plants`. `PlantSpecies` (light, water and heat needs, stiffness, leaf flex, phototropism; `Fern`, `Grass`, `Flower`, `Succulent`, `Palm`, `Pine`), `Soil` (`Sand`, `Loam`, `Clay`: how much it holds and how fast it drains), `PlantState` and `SoilMoisture` advanced by `PlantModel.step` (rain refills the soil, sun and wind dry it, hot sun wilts, too little or too much light stresses, only a healthy plant grows by day, leaves turn toward the sun), `PlantEnvironment.of(sky, wind)`, `PlantPatch` (a fixed bed), `rememberRunningDay` / `TendPlants` (run it on the scene clock) and `drawPlant` (growth, droop and lean around any drawing). A thirsty plant is floppier in the same wind (`PlantSpecies.windResponse(turgor, wetLeaves)`). `DayClock` (day number and hour) added to `boitata-core`; `DirectionalLight.followSun` now uses `SkyModel.SUNRISE` and `SUNSET`, so the sun light and the sky agree. Playground: a Garden tab. See docs/plants.md.
- Stains: a wet container leaves the shape of its foot on what it is set down on. `LiquidVessel.base` (`VesselBase`: a disc, or a ring foot), `bottomWet` / `wetBottom` (wet by what runs over the lip, by standing in a puddle, a cold drink sweats), `SurfaceMaterial` (`Wood`, `Varnished`, `Stone`, `Ceramic`, `Metal`: porosity, permanence, dry rate), `StainField` (a fixed-size pool of marks: `touch`, `deposit`, `step`; a foot set down on its own mark adds to it; coffee and wine stay on wood, a water ring dries away), `SetDown` (finds the moment a moved container comes to rest) and `drawStains`. Playground: surface chips and Wipe stains on the Liquids tab.
- The story, "O Fogo que Guarda": the playground opens on a cordel in nine chapters (`docs/story/boitata-story.md`). A cover with the chapters as bookmarks, each chapter running a scene with its verse on a ribbon (Portuguese, English under it), then pausing into that scene's own controls on a carved paper drawer with a "ficha tecnica" note ("Mexa na cena"); the theatre's paper screen burns away into the forest. New scenes: the festa (every flame a light: bonfire, lanterns, balloon, rockets; flags in the wind), the shot (the muzzle flash is a light that throws every shadow on the wall; smoke and ash on the wind), the chase (the serpent follows the hunter's path through the depth layers, then circles in) and the things that burn on a table (match, candle, paper, rag, lamp).
- Art: 340 woodcut pictures and 17 four-pose animation strips for the story. `VesselArt` lets `drawVessel` use a drawn back, front and shine around the simulated liquid; `PaneArt` gives `drawPaneWeather` and `drawSillSnow` hand-drawn frost ferns, beads and snow. The bench, window, theatre and pond use them.
- Sound: `Gunshot` in `boitata-audio-synth` (crack, boom, echo off the hills; quieter and duller with distance).
- Playground: woodcut people in the cordel style, Lampião, Maria Bonita and a caçador, replace the plain figure. Lampião carries a lampião, the kerosene lamp his nickname comes from, and it is the Depth scene's light. Eight Brazilian animals to place about: onça-pintada, tamanduá-bandeira, capivara, lobo-guará, jacaré, tatu, arara and tucano. `docs/design/ink_cutout.py` turns an exported drawing into a sprite.
- Several lights (`LightRig`, `Lights` presets, `PointLight`, `SpotLight`, `DirectionalLight`): each lights in its own colour and throws its own shadow, as dark as its share of all the light; `drawCastShadows`, `drawLightPools`, `Lights.sun(hour)` and `Lights.moon(hour, phase)` follow the sky. Shadow geometry is pure (`ShadowMath`). See docs/lighting.md.
- Things block light and take shadows: `Billboard` (a standing thing or a wall) with `Optics` (opacity, tint, scatter, blur, distortion, haze; `Opaque`, `Glass`, `Frosted`, `Paper`) and an `AlphaMask`. A thing between a light and another puts it in shadow, by what its optics stop (`Transmission`); `drawFaceShadows` lays the shadows of the things in front of a thing across its face, cut to its outline. `Wall` is now a `Billboard`.
- Light through paper and glass: a `Billboard` with `Optics.Paper()` (or `Frosted`, `Glass`) lit from behind glows with the light that gets through (scattered and tinted by it) and shows the shadows of what is between it and the lamp, enlarged by distance and sharp where they touch it (`ShadowMath.onWallFromPoint` now works for a light on either side; `Billboard.maxShadowScale`; `drawWallLight(gain)`; `drawCastShadows(floor = false)`). Playground: a Shadow theatre tab (Teatro de Sombras).
- Glass that changes the view: `Optics` `blur`, `distortion`, `haze`, `tint` and `scatter` on the Window room's pane (clean, dirty, frosted, old and stained glass): the sky is blurred, bent and veiled, the pane is dirty, and the light patch is tinted, dimmed and softened. Playground: glass chips on the Window tab and a `window-dirty` clip.
- Water: `Liquid` (a pond with a depth profile), `LiquidMath` (Fresnel reflectance, Snell's apparent depth), `drawWaterSurface`, `drawReflection` (mirrored, rippling, fading), `drawStandingInWater` (the submerged part refracted, tinted and shaken, with rings), `drawGlitter`. Playground: a Pond tab.
- Liquids: `LiquidVessel` and `Fluid` (water, tea, wine, milk, oil, kerosene) with a rippling, sloshing surface (`WaveSurface`), a container that tips (`TippingBody`, `Tilt`), spilling over the lip at the rate of a weir with volume kept, `Puddle` (spreads to a settled thickness), and spilled kerosene that burns (`Material.SpilledOil`). Drawing: `drawVessel` (level liquid, ripples, the view behind magnified and tinted, mirrored on the table), `drawSpillStream`, `drawPuddle`, `drawCaustic` (a coloured shadow and a caustic beside a filled glass). Playground: a Liquids tab.
- Weather on glass: `PaneWeather` and `SurfaceClimate` (fog from the dew point, ice growing from the edges, rain beads that clear streaks, snow on the sill, heat, wiping), `drawPaneWeather`, `drawSillSnow`; the Window room turns the pane's state into blur, haze and scatter. `RunOnSceneClock` steps any simulation on the scene's clock.
- Hot liquids and the table edge: `LiquidVessel.temperature` (cooling, mixing by volume on a pour, `heat`), `steam`, `boil` (rising, bursting bubbles), `foam`; `Fluid.Coffee`; `VesselShape.Mug` (ceramic, the surface seen through the opening); `Drips` and `Puddle.remove`: a puddle that reaches the end of a table runs off in drops that fall to a puddle on the floor. Playground: a mug of hot coffee, Pour hot coffee and Heat it, a table with ends and a floor.
- Settable liquids: `Fluid` takes `viscosity`, `density` (kg/m^3), `index`, `turbidity`, `flammable`, and `copy(...)` makes variations; new presets `Honey` and `Mercury` (`Fluid.All`); `LiquidVessel.fluid` can be changed. Density weighs on the container (`TippingBody.setLoad`: a dense liquid lowers the centre of mass, so the glass is harder to knock over); heat thins a thick liquid (`effectiveViscosity`). A puddle that spreads past the front or the back of a table runs off it too, not only the ends; a glass's caustic is kept to the table's top. Playground: liquid chips and viscosity, density, cloudiness and refraction sliders for the picked glass.
- Shadows land on walls: `Wall` in the rig, so a shadow thrown toward a back wall or the face of a crate climbs it, enlarged by distance, instead of lying on the floor; `drawWallLight`.
- Materials `Person` (burns to ash) and `Spirit` (the Boitatá's own flame: never burns down, rain cannot touch it).
- Playground: Lights and Light stage tabs (with a Walls mode), animal walk and flight strips, the Boitatá goes after the hunter and circles him until he is ash, the hunter carries a lamp, and a window that can be shut so the wind stops reaching the plants.
- Fire that spreads: `Material` (ignition heat, burn time, flame size, living or not, and a `BurnStyle`; presets paper, dry grass, cloth, wood, plant, palm, pine, wax, wick, plastic, metal, campfire, none), `FireBody`, and `FireWorld`, a fixed-step simulation where a burning thing heats what stands near it, the wind carries the heat downwind and holds it back upwind, sparks fly in a strong wind, heat builds slowly and cools again, a thing catches, chars, burns out to ash, and spreads on; rain wets and puts fires out; `FireKind.Guardian` (the Boitatá's fire) never lights a living plant and puts out ordinary fires. `RunFireWorld` steps it on the scene clock, `drawBurningBody` draws flames and smoke, and `FireBody.tint` warms and chars a thing's colour.
- Materials react to fire: `BurnStyle` `Char`, `AshAway`, `Shrivel`, `Smoulder`, `Melt`, `Glow`, `None`. `drawFireAffected` draws any picture accordingly (burning edge and wind-blown ash flakes, sag and puddle, ember cracks, heat glow), `BurnStates` replaces any stage with your own art, and `Material` is open to your own presets. Things of several materials are configuration (`FireWorld.addComposite`, `PartSpec`, `PartRegion`): a candle's wick burns while its wax slowly melts. `WindResponse.Ash`.
- Playground: a Burn tab (drag things to a campfire; wind, rain, the Boitatá's fire, start again) and a time-lapse clip.
- Wind: `Wind` (speed, direction, gustiness, gust period, turbulence, travel time; `Calm`, `Breeze`, `Windy`, `Gale`) and `WindResponse` (Free, Anchored or Rigid, with `weight`, `surface`, `stiffness` and a plain `influence`; presets for rain, snow, leaves, embers, dust, fog, clouds, grass, plants, palms, pines, trunks and flames). `BoitataScene(wind = ...)` gives it to everything: rain slants, snow is carried, fog and clouds drift by the distance the air has travelled (so a change of wind never makes them jump), flames lean, embers and sparks streak, window dust blows, and the wind sound swells with the gusts. `sway(...)` draws anything leaning about its foot.
- Breaking: `Rain` and `Snow` no longer take a `wind: Float`; they take `wind: Wind?` and a `response`, and read the scene's wind by default.
- The moon: `MoonPhase` (the eight named phases), `SkyModel.at(hour, weather, MoonPhase)`, `MoonShape.litPath` (a real terminator, tested by counting lit pixels), and `SkyWindow(southernHemisphere = ...)`, which mirrors the lit side.
- `depthDrag` takes an `onTap`.
- `SkyArt` and `StarAtlas`: give `SkyWindow(art = ...)` your own pictures for the sun, the moon (its phase still shapes it) and the stars, drawn as solid shapes with the detail cut out; each is tinted by the sky. The playground uses the woodcut sun, moon and stars from the pen file in every scene.
- Depth: `DepthStage` and `DepthItem` (things on a ground that can be moved about: scale from `Perspective`, draw order, hit test, kept on the ground, a lift while held), `Modifier.depthDrag` in `boitata-scene`, and `Depth` (a single 0..1 depth turned into a size, a fade and a layer slot).
- `drawFlameTrail` takes `scales`, `visibility` and a `range`, so a flame body can pass through depth and be drawn a piece at a time between layers; the glow thins as the body grows so it does not burn out.
- Playground: a Depth tab (drag things about; the lantern is the light), and the serpent swims back to the horizon, between the trees and in front of the foliage.
- Sound (new modules `boitata-audio-synth` and `boitata-audio`): `StormCues` (thunder at `strike.at + thunderDelay`) and `AmbienceLevels` in core; a deterministic synth (`Thunder`, `Ambience.rain/wind/fire`); `AudioSink`, a sample-accurate `SoftwareMixer`, `SceneAudioScheduler` and `BoitataAudio { }`, which schedule sounds on the device's clock from the scene's time, cancel on weather changes and seeks, and pause with the app. Sinks: desktop (`SourceDataLine`) and web (Web Audio); Android and iOS are silent placeholders for now. `RecordingAudioSink` for tests.
- The clock gains `realSeconds` and `inForeground` (thunder keeps going under reduced motion).
- `BoitataScene` and `SceneClock`: one frame loop for a whole scene, shared by every effect, paused when the screen is not in the foreground and still under reduced motion (Android, iOS, web; force it with `SceneConfig`).
- `Quality` (High, Medium, Low) and an adaptive `QualityGovernor`: rain, snow, fog, fire, embers, window dust, shadows and sampling scale with it, and a scene steps down after about a second of slow frames.
- `drawImageSmooth`: the one way to place a moving picture, with a float translation.
- `@AdvancedBoitataApi` on the low-level `draw*` functions; the composables are the safe path.
- `boitata-testing` (`measureScene`) and `checkPerformanceRules` (fails the build on a second frame loop, whole-pixel placement, bicubic sampling, `delay` loops, JVM-only code in common code).
- Breaking: `Rain`, `Snow`, `Fog`, `Fire`, `WindowLight`, `SkyWindow` take `modifier` first and read time and quality from the scene clock; `seconds` and `quality` are optional overrides. `rememberFlicker` follows the clock.
- Fix: `CastSilhouette` and `WallShadow` sampled bicubic (about three times the cost for no visible gain); now mipmapped.
- Fire (`Fire`, `drawFlameTrail`, `fireLight`), cast shadows from any drawing (`CastShadow`), window light (`WindowLight`), rain, snow and fog.
- Playground app (desktop and web), offscreen clip renderer, README GIFs, art style guide.
- `SkyWindow(horizon = ...)`: where the horizon stands, so the same sky works as a window above a desk or as a whole scene.
- Fix: the sky's layers and clouds no longer step a whole pixel at a time. They were placed with integer offsets, so a slow parallax sat still for over a second and then jumped; they now move with float translations, and use mipmapped filtering (about three times cheaper per frame).
- Woodcut art for the playground (`samples/playground/.../composeResources`), the art style guide and the prompts that made it.
- First extraction from The Typist: `boitata-core`, `boitata-scene`, `boitata-light`, `boitata-weather`.

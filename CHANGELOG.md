# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Playground: woodcut people in the cordel style, Lampião, Maria Bonita and a caçador, replace the plain figure. Lampião carries a lampião, the kerosene lamp his nickname comes from, and it is the Depth scene's light. Eight Brazilian animals to place about: onça-pintada, tamanduá-bandeira, capivara, lobo-guará, jacaré, tatu, arara and tucano. `docs/design/ink_cutout.py` turns an exported drawing into a sprite.
- Fire that spreads: `Material` (ignition heat, burn time, flame size, living or not, and a `BurnStyle`; presets paper, dry grass, cloth, wood, plant, palm, pine, wax, wick, plastic, metal, campfire, none), `FireBody`, and `FireWorld`, a fixed-step simulation where a burning thing heats what stands near it, the wind carries the heat downwind and holds it back upwind, sparks fly in a strong wind, heat builds slowly and cools again, a thing catches, chars, burns out to ash, and spreads on; rain wets and puts fires out; `FireKind.Guardian` (the Boitatá's fire) never lights a living plant and puts out ordinary fires. `RunFireWorld` steps it on the scene clock, `drawBurningBody` draws flames and smoke, and `FireBody.tint` warms and chars a thing's colour.
- Materials react to fire: `BurnStyle` `Char`, `AshAway`, `Shrivel`, `Smoulder`, `Melt`, `Glow`, `None`. `drawFireAffected` draws any picture accordingly (burning edge and wind-blown ash flakes, sag and puddle, ember cracks, heat glow), `BurnStates` replaces any stage with your own art, and `Material` is open to your own presets. Things of several materials are configuration (`FireWorld.addComposite`, `PartSpec`, `PartRegion`): a candle's wick burns while its wax slowly melts. `WindResponse.Ash`.
- Playground: a Burn tab (drag things to a campfire; wind, rain, the Boitatá's fire, start again) and a time-lapse clip.
- Wind: `Wind` (speed, direction, gustiness, gust period, turbulence, travel time; `Calm`, `Breeze`, `Windy`, `Gale`) and `WindResponse` (Free, Anchored or Rigid, with `weight`, `surface`, `stiffness` and a plain `influence`; presets for rain, snow, leaves, embers, dust, fog, clouds, grass, plants, palms, pines, trunks and flames). `BoitataScene(wind = ...)` gives it to everything: rain slants, snow is carried, fog and clouds drift by the distance the air has travelled (so a change of wind never makes them jump), flames lean, embers and sparks streak, window dust blows, and the wind sound swells with the gusts. `sway(...)` draws anything leaning about its foot.
- Breaking: `Rain` and `Snow` no longer take a `wind: Float`; they take `wind: Wind?` and a `response`, and read the scene's wind by default.
- The moon: `MoonPhase` (the eight named phases), `SkyModel.at(hour, weather, MoonPhase)`, `MoonShape.litPath` (a real terminator, tested by counting lit pixels), and `SkyWindow(southernHemisphere = ...)`, which mirrors the lit side.
- `depthDrag` takes an `onTap`.
- `SkyArt` and `StarAtlas`: give `SkyWindow(art = ...)` your own pictures for the sun, the moon (its phase still shapes it) and the stars, drawn as solid shapes with the detail cut out; each is tinted by the sky. The playground uses the woodcut sun, moon and stars from the pen file in every scene.
- Depth: `DepthStage` and `DepthItem` (things on a ground that can be moved about: scale from `Perspective`, draw order, hit test, kept on the ground, a lift while held), `Modifier.depthDrag` in `boitata-scene`, and `Depth` (a single 0..1 depth turned into a size, a fade and a layer slot).
- `drawFlameTrail` takes `scales`, `visibility` and a `range`, so a flame body can pass through depth and be drawn a piece at a time between layers; the glow thins as the body grows so it does not burn out.
- Playground: a Depth tab (drag things about; the lantern is the light), and the serpent swims back to the horizon, between the trees and in front of the foliage.
- Sound (new modules `boitata-audio-synth` and `boitata-audio`): `StormCues` (thunder at `strike.at + thunderDelay`) and `AmbienceLevels` in core; a deterministic synth (`Thunder`, `Ambience.rain/wind/fire`); `AudioSink`, a sample-accurate `SoftwareMixer`, `SceneAudioScheduler` and `BoitataAudio { }`, which schedule sounds on the device's clock from the scene's time, cancel on weather changes and seeks, and pause with the app. Sinks: desktop (`SourceDataLine`) and web (Web Audio); Android and iOS are silent placeholders for now. `RecordingAudioSink` for tests.
- The clock gains `realSeconds` and `inForeground` (thunder keeps going under reduced motion).
- `BoitataScene` and `SceneClock`: one frame loop for a whole scene, shared by every effect, paused when the screen is not in the foreground and still under reduced motion (Android, iOS, web; force it with `SceneConfig`).
- `Quality` (High, Medium, Low) and an adaptive `QualityGovernor`: rain, snow, fog, fire, embers, window dust, shadows and sampling scale with it, and a scene steps down after about a second of slow frames.
- `drawImageSmooth`: the one way to place a moving picture, with a float translation.
- `@AdvancedBoitataApi` on the low-level `draw*` functions; the composables are the safe path.
- `boitata-testing` (`measureScene`) and `checkPerformanceRules` (fails the build on a second frame loop, whole-pixel placement, bicubic sampling, `delay` loops, JVM-only code in common code).
- Breaking: `Rain`, `Snow`, `Fog`, `Fire`, `WindowLight`, `SkyWindow` take `modifier` first and read time and quality from the scene clock; `seconds` and `quality` are optional overrides. `rememberFlicker` follows the clock.
- Fix: `CastSilhouette` and `WallShadow` sampled bicubic (about three times the cost for no visible gain); now mipmapped.
- Fire (`Fire`, `drawFlameTrail`, `fireLight`), cast shadows from any drawing (`CastShadow`), window light (`WindowLight`), rain, snow and fog.
- Playground app (desktop and web), offscreen clip renderer, README GIFs, art style guide.
- `SkyWindow(horizon = ...)`: where the horizon stands, so the same sky works as a window above a desk or as a whole scene.
- Fix: the sky's layers and clouds no longer step a whole pixel at a time. They were placed with integer offsets, so a slow parallax sat still for over a second and then jumped; they now move with float translations, and use mipmapped filtering (about three times cheaper per frame).
- Woodcut art for the playground (`samples/playground/.../composeResources`), the art style guide and the prompts that made it.
- First extraction from The Typist: `boitata-core`, `boitata-scene`, `boitata-light`, `boitata-weather`.
